When a language dies, a culture fades, and when culture fades, we lose who we are. At [ndaab](https://www.ndaab.com), we reconnect you to your roots.

## Why We Exist
More than just a language app, we incorporate stories, songs, proverbs, and visual content like cartoons to bring African heritage to life. 
Across the continent and diaspora, many people are losing touch with their native languages and cultures. **ndaab** was born from a vision passed down by our founders' father—who led the [*Cameroon Center for Music Arts and Culture (CCEMAC)*](https://web.facebook.com/ccemac2010?_rdc=1&_rdr#)—to use technology as a tool to revive what’s fading.

## Our Current Focus
- Building MVP for early testers
- Launching core language modules
- Partnering with linguists, communities & cultural organizations
- Preparing for open beta

## Tech Stack
- Frontend: Next.js
- Mobile: React Native
- Backend: Flask / Django / FastAPI
- AI/ML: Python, TensorFlow, OpenAI APIs
- Cloud: AWS (Credits supported)
- Data: PostgreSQL
